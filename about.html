About REDCap Africa Symposium

The REDCap Africa Symposium brings together researchers, data managers and technologists to foster collaboration, share expertise, and improve data quality for health research across the continent.

Welcome from the Chair

Mapule Nhlapho

Chair

It is a pleasure to welcome you to the REDCap Africa Symposium. This gathering brings together researchers, data professionals, developers, and institutions from across the continent to connect, share knowledge, and learn from one another. We hope the symposium creates a valuable space for meaningful conversations and new ideas.

The symposium is an opportunity to celebrate the growing REDCap community in Africa and the important work being done across research and data management. Through presentations, discussions, and collaboration, we aim to encourage the exchange of practical experiences and innovative approaches that can strengthen research across the region.

We are delighted to welcome both returning participants and those joining us for the first time. We hope your time at the symposium is informative, engaging, and filled with opportunities to build new connections. Thank you for being part of this community, and we look forward to sharing a memorable experience together.

Organizing Committee

The symposium is led by a dedicated committee from universities and research institutions across South Africa and the region.
